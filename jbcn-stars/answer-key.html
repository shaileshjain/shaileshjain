18. A. If I is true, II is false, so Bina is the tallest, which contradicts I. If only II is true, Chetan must be the tallest but III being false makes him the shortest, a contradiction. So only III is true: Bina is the tallest, then Chetan, then Asha.

23. C (150°). Angles around point A: 360° − 120° (hexagon) − 90° (square) = 150°.
24. D (9). 4!/2! = 12 arrangements of 2, 2, 0, 5. Those starting with 0 number 3!/2! = 3, leaving 12 − 3 = 9.

27. D (20). Choose any 3 of the 6 points: (6 × 5 × 4) ÷ (3 × 2 × 1) = 20.
28. E (+1). The first column gives the magic sum: −3 + 4 − 1 = 0. One diagonal gives the centre: −3 + centre + 3 = 0, so the centre is 0. The other diagonal: Y + 0 − 1 = 0, so Y = 1.
29. B (4). Dev–Chetan–Esha (in either order) form a block with Chetan in the middle. With Asha in seat 1, the block fills seats 2–4 (Bina in seat 5) or seats 3–5 (Bina in seat 2). That is 2 positions × 2 orders = 4.
30. A (41). Multiples of 3: 33. Multiples of 5: 20. Multiples of both (15): 6. Answer: 33 + 20 − 2 × 6 = 41.
