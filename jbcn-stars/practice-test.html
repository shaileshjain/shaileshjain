TEAM ROUND
Level: JuniorPattern: Problem solving
Maximum marks: 100Duration 60 minutes

Instructions:

  • This document consists of 10 questions in increasing order of difficulty.
  • The team has 60 minutes to discuss and solve them.
  • Each question carries 10 marks. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.
  • Keep your voices soft while discussing.
  • Use blank sheets for working of any kind.
  • Calculators are not permitted.
  • Give answers in the exact form or as specified (include units).
  • At the 55-minute reminder, the team captain uses the last 5 minutes to write the answers on the response sheet.

Requirements:

Response sheet, pen, pencil, eraser and scratch paper

On competition day the Junior level does the Team Round first (08:00–09:15), then the Individual Round (09:35–10:45) after the snack break. Practise in the same order.

Team Round – Junior Level

1. Riya sold 40 bookmarks and Kabir sold 60 bookmarks. All the bookmarks were sold at the same price. Together they received ₹750. How much money did Kabir receive?
2. The volume of a cube is 512 cm3. What is the total surface area of the cube?
3. In a football league a team gets 3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw and 0 points for a loss. At the end of the season, Pi United had 52 points after playing 30 games. They drew 7 games. How many games did they lose?
4. The sum of the squares of two consecutive positive whole numbers is 313. What is the sum of the two numbers?
5. A bus is carrying 54 people, all men or women. When 6 men get off, the ratio of men to women on the bus becomes 1 : 3. How many women are on the bus?
6. A snail is at the bottom of a pole 20 m tall. Every day it climbs up 4 m, and every night it slips down 3 m. On which day does the snail first reach the top of the pole?
7. Pipes A and B can fill a tank in 4 hours and 6 hours respectively. Pipe C can empty the full tank in 8 hours. If all three pipes are opened together on an empty tank, how long will it take to fill the tank? Give your answer in hours and minutes, rounded to the nearest minute.
8. The average age of a group of children is 12.5 years. A teacher aged 38 years joins the group, and the average age of the group rises to 14 years. How many children were in the group?
9. a and b are positive whole numbers with a < b and
a + ab + b = 34
Find a + b.
10. ABCD is a square of side 14 cm. Two quarter circles of radius 14 cm are drawn inside the square, one with centre B and one with centre D. Find the area of the shaded leaf-shaped region where they overlap. (Take π = 227.)

Response sheet, pen, pencil, eraser and scratch paper

Tip: with −1 for a wrong answer, guess only when you can rule out at least two options.
